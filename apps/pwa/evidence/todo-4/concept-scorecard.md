# Todo 4 Concept Drafts And Scorecard

These are text-only concept packets because no image-generation tool is available. They are not represented as Imagen output.

## Concept A: Warm trust-first control desk

- Atmosphere: warm paper workspace held inside a warm-charcoal frame.
- Type: local editorial serif for hierarchy, local humanist sans for controls, local mono for code only.
- Signature: a continuity rail whose label, icon, line treatment, and action make state legible without color.
- Layout: compact app shell, content scroll owner, bottom-reachable action zone, sparse ring depth.
- Color: parchment, ink, clay accent, moss success, ochre warning, brick danger, accessible blue focus.
- Motion: press and dialog state transitions only; no automatic decoration.

## Concept B: Dark developer-native console

- Atmosphere: warm graphite field with tightly separated command surfaces.
- Type: local grotesk and mono-forward hierarchy.
- Signature: a thin command rail and dense tool-result blocks.
- Layout: higher density, split-pane bias at tablet and desktop, bottom composer on mobile.
- Color: charcoal, ash, amber accent, semantic status tones.
- Motion: subtle transform feedback and panel transitions.

## Concept C: High-contrast accessible field kit

- Atmosphere: near-white and near-black utility surface with minimal containment.
- Type: large system sans hierarchy with mono restricted to code.
- Signature: oversized state banners and strongly separated decisions.
- Layout: single-column first, explicit action zones, minimal decorative depth.
- Color: monochrome base, cobalt action, semantic status tones.
- Motion: nearly static with immediate state replacement.

## Weighted scorecard

Each criterion is scored from 1 to 5. Weighted total is out of 100.

| Criterion | Weight | Concept A | Concept B | Concept C |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Task completion | 30 | 5 | 4 | 4 |
| Status legibility | 25 | 5 | 4 | 5 |
| One-handed reach | 20 | 5 | 4 | 4 |
| Accessibility | 15 | 5 | 4 | 5 |
| Non-generic brand fidelity | 10 | 4 | 5 | 3 |
| Weighted total | 100 | 98 | 82 | 86 |

## Selection

Concept A is selected. It best reconciles the locked Claude-derived warmth with operational density and explicit safety states. Concept C contributes its large decision framing and contrast discipline. Concept B contributes compact code/tool anatomy only; its dark-console dominance is not adopted.
